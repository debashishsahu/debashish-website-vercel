## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** Hiring managers, design directors, and senior recruiters evaluating Debashish Sahu for **senior individual-contributor product-design roles** (Staff / Sr. Staff / Principal). They arrive from LinkedIn, a referral, or a job application, often mid-pipeline, and typically skim on desktop first with a deeper read later — some on mobile. They decide in roughly 60–120 seconds whether to go deeper or move on, and they are specifically checking whether the *current* scope of work matches the seniority claimed.

**Secondary:** Design peers and prospective teammates doing due diligence; conference/community organizers pulling a bio.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents Debashish's product-design practice and body of work, and converts a qualified visitor into an interview conversation. Success = a hiring manager for a senior IC role finishes a short visit convinced the work backs the positioning, and reaches out (or advances the candidate) via a low-friction channel.

## Product Principles

1. **Match the evidence to the claim.** The prominence of a piece of work should track how well it supports the senior-enterprise-AI positioning — recency and relevance lead, nostalgia doesn't.
2. **Honesty over polish.** Cite only real, sourced metrics and quotes. Mark older work as foundational rather than dressing it up as current. Never reconstruct NDA'd work without confirmation.
3. **The site is a work sample.** Accessibility, responsive behavior, and resilience without JS are non-negotiable because the audience is judging craft.
4. **Respect a 90-second read.** The first screen and the work index must let a hiring manager reach a correct, favorable judgment fast; depth rewards the second visit.
5. **One obvious way to make contact.** Every page offers a single low-friction path to start a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: text and meaningful non-text contrast ≥ the AA thresholds across every theme; full keyboard operability with a visible focus indicator; correct heading order with one `<h1>` per page; `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (no opacity-gated content, no infinite motion); content legible with JavaScript degraded or blocked. Corporate-managed, older, and throttled browsers are in scope.
